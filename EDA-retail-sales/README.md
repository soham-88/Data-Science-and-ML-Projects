## 📌 About the Project

This project performs a comprehensive EDA on a retail sales dataset to uncover patterns, trends, and customer insights that help retail businesses make informed decisions.

The analysis goes beyond basic EDA — it includes **RFM analysis** (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) to segment customers into actionable groups: Champions, Loyal Customers, Potential Loyalists, At Risk, and Lost Customers.

Built as part of my **Data Analytics Internship at Oasis Infobyte**.

### 8. 🏷️ Customer Segmentation — RFM Analysis

**RFM** is a proven marketing model that segments customers based on three dimensions:

| Dimension | Definition |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Recency (R)** | Days since the customer's last purchase |
| **Frequency (F)** | Total number of transactions |
| **Monetary (M)** | Total revenue generated by the customer |

Each customer receives a score from 1–4 for each dimension, combined into an RFM Score, then mapped to 5 segments:

| Segment | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 🏆 Champions | Bought recently, buy often, spend the most |
| 💙 Loyal Customers | Regular buyers with strong monetary value |
| 🌱 Potential Loyalists | Recent buyers with growing frequency |
| ⚠️ At Risk | Past high-value customers who haven't returned |
| ❌ Lost Customers | Inactive, low engagement, lowest scores |

**Outputs:** Segment count bar chart + Average Monetary Value per Segment chart
